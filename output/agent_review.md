# Working with agents: a recorded case study

A real Claude run on synthetic data, with illustrative analyst instructions. Replayed locally against the same inputs; not a live employer review. Final approval remains open.

## The assignment

Investigate the FY2025 provisional Brand Events operating loss. Use the available financial tools to check the result, relevant business notes and operating drivers. Identify the facts a manager needs and the questions that remain open.

## Company context

Events agency. Four business lines deliver client projects using employees and external suppliers.

The agents receive the company metric definitions, reporting date and review rules. Billings are invoiced amounts; rate and price/mix residuals do not establish causes.

## What the investigation agent checked

- Checked the provisional annual scorecard.
- Requested headcount/rate and project-volume/mix breakdowns.
- Examined material variances and their evidence references.
- Read dated business notes: N11, N13.
### Analyst follow-up

Keep the November billings placeholder separate from business performance. Do not explain rate or price/mix residuals as proven causes. Check the relevant source notes before proposing project recovery actions. Make the final brief useful to the BU manager; no source correction or forecast adjustment is approved.
