## What drove it

- **Payroll ran -30k vs budget.** Headcount effect -26k (average 54.6 FTE vs 55.0 planned), rate effect -4.5k (salary mix, overtime and timing). The two effects reconcile exactly to the payroll variance.
- **Net billings ran -254k vs budget (excluding the month pending data correction).** Volume effect +1,494k (146 projects delivered vs 141 planned), price/mix effect -1,748k. The two effects reconcile exactly to the net billings variance.
- *Data note:* 2025-11 is excluded from the revenue split above. The ops system shows normal activity that month (17 projects delivered vs 17 planned), which supports treating the recorded revenue figure as a data entry error rather than a business event.
