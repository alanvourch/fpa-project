Rolling forecast: Q3 2026

As of 30 June 2026. Synthetic provisional reporting scenario.

Method

Forecast = same-month-prior-year actual times median year-over-year growth over the last 12 months, by business unit and P&L line. Unresolved business effects remain in history. A single material month is not evidence of a one-off; an episode ending does not establish that a structural effect has stopped.

Only a confirmed one-off with approved status, reviewer, date, evidence and an explicit replacement in data/forecast_reviews.csv changes history. Proposed adjustments remain proposals. Structural effects and timing shifts are retained pending an explicit future schedule. Missing source cells and the November billings placeholder follow the separate provisional reporting policy; neither is a confirmed accounting value.

Historical evaluation

Missing or flagged target source cells are excluded. The synthetic, short and overlapping sample does not establish performance on real employer data.
