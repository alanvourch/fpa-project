EventCo is a fictional events agency with four business lines. Reporting date: 30 June 2026. Historical review: FY2025; outlook: July-September 2026.

Purpose and approach

From source files to a management review

The goal is to make recurring reporting easier to prepare and review. I designed the finance rules and workflow, then built the scripts and agent instructions with AI assistance.

  1. 1 · Source files

    Finance and operating data

    Monthly finance exports, business notes, headcount and project volumes.

  2. 2 · Python automation

    Calculate and check

    Clean the inputs, calculate variances and forecasts, and reconcile the figures.

  3. 3 · Claude agents

    Investigate and draft

    Choose relevant checks, read supporting notes and prepare a short management brief.

  4. 4 · Analyst review

    Challenge and decide

    Investigate gaps, judge assumptions and select actions before approving the report.

An agent is an AI assistant with a defined assignment and a limited set of checks it can request. Python supplies the figures; the analyst remains responsible for the conclusions.

How the agents learn the company's reporting rules

Both roles receive a short company brief: how the business earns money, what each metric means, the reporting period and what needs analyst approval. For EventCo, billings are invoiced amounts; a rate or price/mix residual does not establish a cause. Missing source figures must stay visible.

The investigation agent can request scorecards, operating-driver breakdowns and dated notes. The reporting agent selects the useful facts and proposes questions for the manager. An agent's suggestion remains subject to review.

The company brief and role instructions are maintained separately from the calculations. Formatting follows a report template. Read the synthetic company brief.

Method and review

What runs locally, and what needs judgment

  1. Clean dates, names and currency formatting. Keep missing source amounts blank and flag unusual budget ratios for verification.
  2. Compute material variances, link relevant dated notes and record analyst explanations separately. Keep unresolved items open.
  3. Build the provisional forecast, report the assumptions and compare historical holdouts. Require explicit recurrence review before changing history.
  4. Render business-line reports and scoped executive statements. Bind each statement to its unit, period, metric, signed amount and evidence.
  5. Run validation and assemble a draft. A failed required check returns a failing status and blocks the site build. Changed artifacts require validation again.

Data definitions

Net billings are invoiced amounts. Gross margin is billings less project costs. This simplified management P&L does not implement accounting revenue recognition, ARR or ACV restatement, a full close, or SAP/BPC integration.

Data handling

Calculations and default commentary run locally. A live agent run sends the synthetic company brief, facts and selected tool results to Claude. The page shows a recorded run; visiting it makes no AI request. Aggregation does not anonymize business context. Source scans are limited regression checks, not runtime network isolation.

Technical appendix: reproduction, checks and limitations

The input has 120 cleaned BU-month rows over January 2024-June 2026, with 11 P&L lines per row. All data is synthetic. The 13 analyst explanations are illustrative; driver data is consistent by construction.

.venv\Scripts\python.exe orchestrator.py --provisional

Without --provisional, unresolved source gaps stop reporting. The optional scenario records estimates in a separate adjustment ledger; these estimates are not approved actuals or budgets.

Calculations are reproducible for fixed inputs. Commentary uses constrained statements; changing wording requires a template change and review. Validation rejects arbitrary prose and does not claim to understand every possible financial assertion. The agent example replays a recorded Claude conversation and recomputes every financial check against the same inputs. A fresh Claude run may choose different checks or questions. It cannot approve financial changes or publish reports.
