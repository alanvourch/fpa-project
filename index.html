Forecast, assumptions and risks

The selected provisional baseline forecasts EUR23.0M of net billings and EUR663k of operating profit. August shows an operating loss of EUR193k. The seasonal shape does not establish that the loss is acceptable.

A material variance is a reason to investigate. It is removed from history only after a confirmed one-off classification, approval, evidence and an explicit replacement value. Timing shifts and structural effects need a future schedule before an adjustment can be applied.

Four overlapping historical holdouts compare seasonal growth with a seasonal naive forecast. On this synthetic sample, growth has a higher mean absolute error (EUR26k per line-month versus EUR25k). These results support further model review.

Method and review

What runs locally, and what needs judgment

  1. Clean dates, names and currency formatting. Keep missing source amounts blank and flag unusual budget ratios for verification.
  2. Compute material variances, link relevant dated notes and record analyst explanations separately. Keep unresolved items open.
  3. Build the provisional forecast, report the assumptions and compare historical holdouts. Require explicit recurrence review before changing history.
  4. Render business-line reports and scoped executive statements. Bind each statement to its unit, period, metric, signed amount and evidence.
  5. Run validation and assemble a draft. A failed required check returns a failing status and blocks the site build. Changed artifacts require validation again.

Data definitions

Net billings are invoiced amounts. Gross margin is billings less project costs. This simplified management P&L does not implement accounting revenue recognition, ARR or ACV restatement, a full close, or SAP/BPC integration.

Data handling

Calculations and default commentary run locally. Optional AI ordering sends a structured statement catalogue to the configured provider. Aggregation does not anonymize business context. Source scans are limited regression checks, not runtime network isolation.

Technical appendix: reproduction, checks and limitations

The input has 120 cleaned BU-month rows over January 2024-June 2026, with 11 P&L lines per row. All data is synthetic. The 13 analyst explanations are illustrative; driver data is consistent by construction.

Without --provisional, unresolved source gaps stop reporting. The optional scenario records estimates in a separate adjustment ledger; these estimates are not approved actuals or budgets.

Calculations are reproducible for fixed inputs. Commentary uses constrained statements; changing wording requires a template change and review. Validation rejects arbitrary prose and does not claim to understand every possible financial assertion. Optional AI can reorder statements, so wording and ordering are not claimed to be identical across fresh AI calls.
